No events carry a canary field in this batch, so no deliberate plants were present to confirm. The succession outcome is the only mild Rule 1 risk, because of its 'most powerful DPM' superlative. Priority fixes are accuracy issues: GDP described as the 'largest' National Bonus indicator, a Minister in the ranking exercise, an unconditional by-election on resignation, and the service 'volunteering' officers for party-run MPS. Tone fixes are a fourth-wall wink ('the other two options') and a few self-explaining or moralising closers (forum_letter, budget_round, the_tender, cos_speech). Minor items are dated BTO terminology, a He/She continuity break between cos_speech and cos_vindicated, quintile versus 20th-percentile wording, and MX grades placed at statutory boards. Salary figures for MR4, PM and MP are correct. The other political office holder multiples could not be verified.
