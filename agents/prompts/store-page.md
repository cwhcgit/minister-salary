Minister Salary is a text-based career simulator set in Singapore's public service. You join as a junior officer, and every tap is one year of your working life: read the year's event, make a choice, and see what follows, including your annual pay package and how your performance was described. Climb the grades, survive your appraisals, and perhaps one day be invited into politics. The institutions are modelled on the real ones; every character is fictional. Choices are permanent, and some of them come back.
