# Minister Salary: M1 playtest brief (stand-in)

## Design intent

A text career simulator, one tap per year, from junior public servant towards Prime Minister. The core fantasy is climbing a ladder whose rungs you cannot see, and learning the rules only by paying for them. The hidden ceiling (CEP) is the heart of the game: never shown as a number, surfaced only as rumour and euphemism, and reading those euphemisms is the skill the game teaches. Deferred consequences, a choice in year 6 coming back in year 14, are the single most valuable feature. A full run should take 25 to 45 minutes; endings are collectible and replay is the point.

## Tone rules

1. Institutions are real; people are invented. No real politician is depicted, named or caricatured.
2. Punch at machinery, not at persons.
3. Deadpan. The game never winks.
4. Accuracy is the joke: grades, acronyms, formulas and processes must be right.
5. Affection: made by someone who knows the system, not someone sneering at it.

## Balance invariant

PM must stay rare by accident and reachable by skill. The origin ladder (scholar easiest, graduate, then poly hardest) must hold for random and win-seeking play; a content change that inverts it is wrong. The stat-optimiser placing graduate above scholar is accepted.

## Fun criteria (reconstructed)

1. Every year gives the player something worth reading.
2. Every stat is pushed both ways by real choices.
3. No choice is obviously dominant.
4. Choices feel weighty, and the ones that come back are recognised when they do.
5. Losing early happens only through the player's own choices, never bad luck alone.
6. The player wants to start another run when one ends.

## Known gaps (reconstructed; report how much each hurts, not that it exists)

- Flag-driven follow-ups: choices set flags, but almost no event reads them, so most choices never come back.
- Act III (politics) is thin: few one-time events, so years repeat filler.
- Some stats move mostly one way.
- Traits and ministry preference from the design are not built; the ministry is flavour only.
- The Opposition branch is out of scope for v1.

## Open questions

1. Turn length: should Act III play in quarters?
2. Is CEP too cruel? A hard invisible ceiling may read as unfair rather than pointed. The intended safeguard is that at least one CEP-raising event stays reachable.
3. Failure pacing: should a run be losable at year 5? Leaning: losable, but only through active choices.
4. Title: "Minister Salary" is plain; "Confirmed & Substantive" is funnier to insiders and opaque to everyone else.
