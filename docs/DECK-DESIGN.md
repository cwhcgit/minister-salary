# The deck, with the mechanics

Every card in Minister Salary, with the mechanics: who sees each card, what each choice does to the hidden numbers, the follow-ups it can schedule and their chances, who might notice, the decisions cards share, series and Meanwhile lines. The text is exactly as in [the player's view](DECK.md).

Cards are numbered by when a career can first meet them: each start's first card, then by grade, from Management Executive to Prime Minister, then the batchmate's cards and a career's last years, then the endings. Read it as a career: start at card 1 and follow **Next →**, or jump from a choice to what it can lead to. A follow-up card comes right after the first card that can lead to it, so a story reads in order.

One sample career throughout: you are Lim Wei Ling (Wei Ling to colleagues, Ms Lim to officials, Ms Lim Wei Ling on the record), in the Ministry of Health. The people around you are the peer, Xin Yi; the junior officer, Faizal; the reporting officer, Ms Chua; the journalist, Rajesh Nair; the grassroots leader, Mdm Rohani; the fellow MP, Benedict; and the batchmate is Hui Min. A real career draws its own names. Each person is introduced the first time they appear, as the game does.

Under a card, folded: the scenes it has when it comes back, its other versions, and the parts it is put together from.
